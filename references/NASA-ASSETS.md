## 1. Desert / Abydos-like Planets & Dunes

### 1.1 Gale Crater Naukluft Sandstone & Bagnold Dunes (Mars)
- **Planet Match:** Abydos (Surface Vista & Dunes) / P8X-412
- **Descriptive Name:** 360-degree Panorama from the Naukluft Plateau showing ancient windblown sandstone dunes and the Gale Crater rim.
- **Source Mission / Agency:** NASA / JPL-Caltech / MSSS (Curiosity Rover Mastcam)
- **Direct Image Link:** [PIA20332 - Naukluft Plateau Panorama (8703x2000)](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/5/53/PIA20332-fig1-MarsCuriosityRover-NaukluftPlateau-20160404.jpg)
- **Wikimedia Commons Page:** [File:PIA20332-fig1-MarsCuriosityRover-NaukluftPlateau-20160404.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:PIA20332-fig1-MarsCuriosityRover-NaukluftPlateau-20160404.jpg)
- **NASA Catalog:** [PIA20332](https://photojournal.jpl.nasa.gov/catalog/PIA20332)
- **License Status:** Public Domain (`PD-USGov-NASA`)
- **Recommended Usage:** Stargate destination preview banner for Abydos or arid desert worlds; panoramic header for off-world expedition mission logs.

### 1.2 "Eye of the Sahara" (Richat Structure from Orbit)
- **Planet Match:** Abydos Orbital Scan / Buried Alien Ring Structure
- **Descriptive Name:** Orbital photograph of the 40-km wide circular Richat Structure in Mauritania, appearing as an ancient, concentric ringed fortress buried in desert sands.
- **Source Mission / Agency:** NASA / ISS Expedition 42 (`ISS042-E-134149`)
- **Direct Image Link:** [ISS-42 Richat Structure (4928x3280)](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/a/a7/ISS-42_Richat_Structure%2C_Eye_of_the_Sahara.jpg)
- **Wikimedia Commons Page:** [File:ISS-42_Richat_Structure,_Eye_of_the_Sahara.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:ISS-42_Richat_Structure,_Eye_of_the_Sahara.jpg)
- **License Status:** Public Domain (`PD-USGov-NASA`)
- **Recommended Usage:** Dialing Computer orbital reconnaissance screen; planet selection thumbnail for ancient Goa'uld ruins and pyramid landing sites.

## 2. Jungle / Lush Alien Worlds

### 2.1 Kepler-186f Habitable Zone Exoplanet
- **Planet Match:** P3X-974 / Eden / Lush Garden Worlds
- **Descriptive Name:** NASA concept visualization of Kepler-186f, the first validated Earth-size exoplanet orbiting within the habitable zone of an M-dwarf star, depicting liquid water and alien foliage.
- **Source Mission / Agency:** NASA Ames / SETI Institute / JPL-Caltech (Art: T. Pyle, `PIA18000`)
- **Direct Image Link:** [Kepler-186f Artist Concept (4534x2550)](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/c/c1/Kepler186f-ArtistConcept-20140417.jpg)
- **Wikimedia Commons Page:** [File:Kepler186f-ArtistConcept-20140417.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Kepler186f-ArtistConcept-20140417.jpg)
- **License Status:** Public Domain (`PD-USGov-NASA`)
- **Recommended Usage:** Colony establishment target overview screen; SGC Dialing Computer "Habitable / Class-M" destination card.

### 3.1 "Cosmic Cliffs" of the Carina Nebula (JWST NIRCam)
- **Planet Match:** Hyperspace Corridor / Wormhole Event Horizon / Starbirth Nebula
- **Descriptive Name:** Ultra-high-resolution James Webb Space Telescope infrared view revealing the luminous, gas-carved ridges of NGC 3324 with glittering protostars.
- **Source Mission / Agency:** NASA / ESA / CSA / STScI
- **Direct Image Link:** [Webb Cosmic Cliffs (14575x8441)](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/44/NASA%E2%80%99s_Webb_Reveals_Cosmic_Cliffs%2C_Glittering_Landscape_of_Star_Birth.jpg)

## 4. Asgard / Futuristic High-Tech Alien Worlds / Ice Worlds

### 4.1 Chaotic Ridges & Crystalline Fractures of Europa
- **Planet Match:** Othala / Asgard Outpost / Crystalline Ice World
- **Descriptive Name:** High-resolution surface mosaic from Galileo Solid-State Imaging (SSI) showing chaotic crisscrossing pressure ridges, fractures, and reddish subsurface mineral stains across Europa's frozen ocean.
- **Source Mission / Agency:** NASA / JPL
- **Direct Image Link:** [Europa Surface Mosaic (2300x1700)](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/1/1f/Europa%27s_surface.jpg)
- **Wikimedia Commons Page:** [File:Europa's_surface.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Europa%27s_surface.jpg)
- **License Status:** Public Domain (`PD-USGov-NASA` / JPL)
- **Recommended Usage:** Asgard technology cache / subterranean base mission backdrop; arctic world gate target card.

### 4.2 Enceladus Cryovolcanic Plumes (Cassini)
- **Planet Match:** Sub-Zero Outpost / Cryogenic Atmosphere World
- **Descriptive Name:** Iconic backlit view of Saturn's moon Enceladus showing multiple cryo-geysers erupting high into space from the south polar "tiger stripe" fractures.
- **Source Mission / Agency:** NASA / JPL / SSI (Cassini Imaging Science Subsystem, `PIA11688`)
- **Direct Image Link:** [Enceladus Geysers (1580x977)](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/e/e6/Enceladus_geysers_June_2009.jpg)

- **License Status:** Public Domain (`PD-USGov-NASA`)
